The Voice Behind The Movement

About Ladyh Joy O.

Ladyh Joy O. is a mentor, speaker, and founder of the Quiet Reset Movement — an initiative committed to helping leaders, wives, business owners, and ministry servants heal from burnout.

Having walked through heavy seasons of personal loss, ministry disillusionment, and hidden emotional strain, Ladyh writes not from ivory-tower theories, but from the trenches of rebuilding. Her work pairs practical emotional health insights with deep scripture to walk readers out of survival mode and into purposeful peace.

“Pain that was never loud ran deep, but God’s restoration runs deeper.”

Quiet Reset Excerpt

Chapter 1: The Sound of an Unraveling

What happens when the life you built—the success, the ministry, the dreams—starts falling apart, not with a loud crash, but with a quiet unraveling?

No one talks about the silent struggles. The weight of unspoken disappointments. The exhaustion of keeping everything together while something inside you is breaking.

“Did I miss something, Lord? Did I fail? Are You still with me?”

In this season of silence, you may feel abandoned. But what if this silence isn’t an ending? What if God is clearing the stage so that your heart can finally hear His whisper?
