Chapter 1 Excerpt

The Sound of an Unraveling

What happens when the life you built—the success, the ministry, the dreams—starts falling apart, not with a loud crash, but with a quiet unraveling?

No one talks about the silent struggles. The weight of unspoken disappointments. The exhaustion of keeping everything together while something inside you is breaking. The questions you’re too afraid to ask: Did I miss something, Lord? Did I fail? Are You still with me?

We live in a culture that applauds the sprint, that celebrates relentless output, and treats exhaustion like a medal of honor. But your soul was not designed to carry tomorrow's burdens with yesterday's depleted energy.

Maybe this silence is not the end. Maybe this very place of unraveling is God’s invitation to a quiet reset.
