Quiet Reset Excerpt

Chapter 1: The Sound of an Unraveling

By Ladyh Joy O.

What happens when the life you built—the success, the ministry, the dreams—starts falling apart, not with a loud crash, but with a quiet unraveling?

No one talks about the silent struggles. The weight of unspoken disappointments. The exhaustion of keeping everything together while something inside you is breaking. The questions you’re too afraid to ask out loud:

“Did I miss something, Lord? Did I fail? Are You still with me?”

We live in a culture that rewards continuous performance, celebrates round-the-clock grinding, and treats depletion like a trophy. We learn how to put on a smiling face for Sunday services, for meetings, for social media feeds, while our inner spirit is hollowed out.

In this season of silence, you may feel abandoned. But what if this silence isn’t an ending? What if God is clearing the noise so that your heart can finally hear His whisper?
